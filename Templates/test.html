Bijoy to Unicode Converter - Test Page

Test the conversion by pasting Bijoy encoded text directly

Instructions:

  1. Paste Bijoy encoded Bangla text in the left textarea
  2. Click the "Convert to Unicode" button
  3. The converted Unicode text will appear in the right textarea
  4. Use the "Copy Unicode" button to copy the converted text to clipboard

For proper display of Bijoy text, you need to have SutonnyMJ font installed on your computer.

For proper display of Unicode text, you need to have SolaimanLipi or any Unicode compliant Bangla font installed.
